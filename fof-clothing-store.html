Who We Are

Company Profile

F.O.F Clothing — short for Field Of Fashion — is a menswear store built for men who take their wardrobe seriously. We are designers that work with you to produce traditional, formal and casual pieces from quality fabric, cut to your size and made to order.

Every piece on this store is made after an order is placed and payment is verified, so what you get is cut specifically for you — not pulled off a rack. We also take on personal designs and fabric colour changes on request; reach Customer Care on 09020425785 for anything outside the standard catalog.
